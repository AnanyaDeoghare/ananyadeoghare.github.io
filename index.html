01. About me

I am a Senior Associate Algorithm Engineer at QuinStreet, where I own the end-to-end machine learning ranking architecture for the Personal Loans vertical, spanning CTR and conversion modeling, optimal allocation, constrained marketplace optimization, simulation, and online experimentation. Over 5+ years in AdTech and data engineering, I’ve focused on production ML systems where modeling decisions move revenue. Day to day, my work centers on matching consumer intent to the right offers in real time, testing ranking changes offline in a large-scale simulator before any production rollout, and guarding results with experimentation and monitoring. I also help set modeling standards through design reviews.

I hold a Master’s in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UCLA, where my research on equitable remote health sensing was published at SIGGRAPH 2022. Earlier, I spent two years as a Software Engineer at Accenture in India, building data solutions for the healthcare and pharma sectors, and interned in ML at VidMob.

I’m passionate about using technology to drive meaningful impact, and I thrive on transforming complex challenges into practical, human-centered solutions. Outside work, I pursue Bharatanatyam, an Indian classical dance form, and cooking, both of which keep me focused and composed.

FEATURED · 03

Equitable Multimodal Remote Plethysmography · SIGGRAPH 2022

Blending RGB camera video with 77 GHz millimeter-wave radar and a shift-robust loss function to eliminate skin-tone bias in remote heart-rate estimation.

The challenge

Camera-based remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) suffers accuracy drops on darker skin tones due to optical absorption, and unsynchronized camera-oximeter capture introduces temporal lag.

My contribution

Diagnosed skin-tone bias with the UCLA Visual Machines Group, contributed to an adversarial multimodal fusion algorithm combining 77 GHz radar and RGB video, and built a shift-robust loss function in PyTorch for misaligned PPG signals.

Outcomes

  • Improved heart-rate estimation accuracy across diverse skin tones by 75% (published at SIGGRAPH 2022).
  • Decreased rPPG pulse prediction error by 40% using the shift-robust loss function.
  • Wrote 2 chapters on Computational Imaging for an MIT Press textbook.
