FEATURED · 02 · CASE STUDY

Hungarian vs. Greedy Constrained Marketplace Optimization

Replacing greedy offer slotting with global optimal constrained assignment (Hungarian algorithm) validated across staged online A/B rollouts.

Problem & approach

Problem: Greedy offer allocation sub-optimally matched consumer inquiries under advertiser eligibility and budget constraints. Approach: Formulated slotting as a global constrained assignment problem using the Hungarian algorithm to maximize expected marketplace margin across eligible offers.

Validation & next steps

Validation: Replayed against a replica of the live system before release and validated online through staged A/B rollouts (staged 90/10, then 50/50) against the greedy baseline on matched publisher traffic. Next steps: Extending constrained assignment with joint CTR × CVR expected-margin scoring across additional verticals.

Result

  • Delivered a 2.5–8% margin revenue lift in staged online A/B tests vs. greedy baseline.
  • Now live in production across Auto Insurance and Banking.
  • Validated on matched publisher traffic across staged 90/10 and 50/50 rollouts.

FEATURED · 03

Equitable Multimodal Plethysmography · SIGGRAPH 2022

Blending RGB camera video with 77 GHz millimeter-wave radar and a shift-robust loss function to mitigate skin-tone bias in remote heart-rate sensing.

The challenge

Camera-based remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) degrades on darker skin tones due to optical absorption, and unsynchronized camera-oximeter capture introduces temporal lag.

My contribution

Contributed to an adversarial multimodal fusion pipeline combining 77 GHz radar and RGB video at UCLA VMG, and with a fellow researcher derived a shift-robust PyTorch loss function for temporally misaligned PPG signals.

Outcomes

  • Improved heart-rate estimation accuracy across diverse skin tones by 75% (published at SIGGRAPH 2022).
  • Reduced pulse prediction error by 40% with the shift-robust loss function.
