FEATURED · 03

Equitable Multimodal Plethysmography · SIGGRAPH 2022

Blending RGB camera video with 77 GHz millimeter-wave radar and a shift-robust loss function to mitigate skin-tone bias in remote heart-rate sensing.

The challenge

Camera-based remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) degrades on darker skin tones due to optical absorption, and unsynchronized camera-oximeter capture introduces temporal lag.

My contribution

Contributed to an adversarial multimodal fusion pipeline combining 77 GHz radar and RGB video at UCLA VMG, and developed a shift-robust PyTorch loss function for temporally misaligned PPG signals.

Outcomes

  • Improved heart-rate estimation accuracy across diverse skin tones by 75% (published at SIGGRAPH 2022).
  • Reduced pulse prediction error by 40% with the shift-robust loss function.
